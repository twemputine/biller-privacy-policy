Biller privacy policy

Applies to the Biller Android app (com.biller.app) and the Biller web app. Effective 15 October 2026.
In short
  • Biller has no server and no user accounts. Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • The developer never receives your bills, balances, income, payments or any identifier.
  • Data leaves the device only when you choose to export a file, export a calendar file, or connect your own Google account.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

1. What Biller stores

Biller is a bill, debt and payday tracker. You enter the data yourself: bill names, amounts, due dates, categories, balances, interest rates, credit limits, payment history, notes, take-home pay and pay schedule, reminder preferences, and optional settings such as theme and an app-lock PIN. All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device (in the web version, in your browser's storage for the site). It is not transmitted to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

2. What Biller does not do

  • It does not connect to banks, card issuers or lenders, and cannot move money or make payments.
  • It does not access a credit bureau or your credit score.
  • It does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, messages or device identifiers.
  • It does not sell, share or rent any information, because it never has any.

3. Optional Google features

You may sign in with your own Google account to use two optional features. Signing in is never required to use Biller.

FeatureGoogle permission (scope)What Biller does with it
Calendar synchttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendarCreates a calendar named “Biller” in your account and writes one recurring all-day event per bill, plus one for paydays. Biller only reads and writes events it created in that calendar. It does not read your other calendars.
Drive backuphttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdataStores a single backup file, biller-backup.json, in the app-private area of your Google Drive. That area is only visible to Biller and cannot see any other file in your Drive.
Account labelhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.emailShows which account is connected. The address is kept on the device only.

Sign-in happens directly between your device and Google. On Android the app keeps a refresh token on the device so that sync and backup can run silently; disconnecting in Settings deletes it. Google's handling of your account is governed by Google's privacy policy. Biller's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Biller's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Biller calendar and the Drive backup remain in your Google account until you remove them.

4. Exports you create

Backup files (JSON) and calendar files (.ics) are created on request and handed to the app or location you pick in the Android share sheet or browser download. They contain your bill data in plain text. Where you send or store them is under your control.

6. App lock

If you set a PIN, it is stored only as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash, never in clear text. Biometric unlock is delegated to Android; the app receives only a success or failure result.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Settings → Delete all bills removes all bill data; uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes everything, including the PIN hash and the Google sign-in token. Backups you exported and the Drive backup are deleted by you, from wherever you stored them or from Settings → Google → Delete cloud backup. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete or hand over.

8. Children

Biller is a personal-finance tool intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information.

9. Changes

If a future version starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the in-app Help will say so before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top shows the latest revision.
